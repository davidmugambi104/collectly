## What this email does (and doesn't do)

### Does
- Asks for TWO specific actions (try it, upvote it)
- Asks for HONEST feedback, not "support"
- Gives them an out ("if this isn't a fit, no hard feelings")
- Tells them the EXACT upvote time (12:01 AM PT = 8:01 AM EAT)
- Pre-loads the ask for Wednesday with a separate email Tuesday night

### Doesn't
- Beg or sound desperate
- Promise equity / discounts / favors
- Ask for social shares (PH is the only channel that matters on day 1)
- Include a long backstory
- Have a corporate signature

## What if I don't have 5-10 people?

You need them. This is the single highest-leverage thing for PH rank.

**Who to ask (in order of conversion likelihood):**
1. People who have asked "how's the startup going?" in the last 6 months
2. People who have worked at startups before (they get it)
3. People who have ever had a side project (they respect the grind)
4. Friends-of-friends who are 1 degree away

**How to ask them in person (text or voice):**
> "Hey, I'm launching my SaaS on PH on Wednesday at 8 AM my time.
> I have a list of 5-10 people I'm asking for an upvote that morning.
> Can I put you on it? All I need is 30 seconds of your time and
> an honest reaction to the product."

If they say yes, add them to the list. If they say "I'll think about it,"
they mean no. Move on.

**What if I get fewer than 5?**
Send anyway. 3-5 is enough to make a difference, especially if they
each leave a comment. The product has to do the heavy lifting beyond that.

---

## What NOT to do

- ❌ Don't ask for "an upvote and a comment and a share" — too much
- ❌ Don't send a 500-word backstory
- ❌ Don't promise anything in return (it feels transactional)
- ❌ Don't apologize for asking
- ❌ Don't include a calendar link
- ❌ Don't CC anyone (it's not a mass email)
