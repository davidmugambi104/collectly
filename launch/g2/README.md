## Full description

Collectly is the AI-native accounts-receivable platform built for $1M–$20M revenue businesses. It sits on top of QuickBooks or Xero and automates the three jobs that take up the most founder time:

1. **Dunning** — Tone-aware AI (GPT-4o) writes and sends reminder sequences. Friendly → Firm → Final. Auto-pauses on reply or payment.
2. **Cash-flow forecasting** — 4-week projection of incoming cash, with confidence intervals. Know if you can make payroll.
3. **Branded payment portal** — Card, ACH, wire, SEPA, BACS, AU Direct Debit. Mobile-optimized. Subdomain routing.
