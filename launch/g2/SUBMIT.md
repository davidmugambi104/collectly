## STEP 5: Categories where you want to compete

G2 lets you self-select categories. Pick the 2-3 where you can credibly win:

1. **Accounts Receivable Automation** ← primary
2. **Billing & Invoicing** ← secondary
3. **Small Business Accounting** ← tertiary

G2's reviewer algorithm ranks you against other products in the same category. Picking too many dilutes the signal. Stick to 3.

## STEP 7: After submission

G2 will:
1. Email you a verification link
2. Ask for a quick video call to confirm the product works (5-10 min, async or live)
3. Approve in 3-7 business days
4. Email you when you go live

**The video call is the bottleneck.** Schedule it ASAP after submission so it doesn't sit in queue.
