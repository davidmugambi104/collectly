## Full description

Collectly helps small businesses get paid faster. It automates the part of accounts receivable that founders hate: chasing late invoices.

**Core features:**

- **AI Dunning Engine** — GPT-4o writes tone-aware reminder emails and SMS. Friendly, firm, or final. Auto-pauses when the customer replies or pays.
- **Cash-Flow Forecast** — 4-week projection of incoming cash. Know exactly when payroll is safe.
- **AR Aging Dashboard** — Real-time buckets (Current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+). Drill into a customer, see every invoice.
- **Branded Payment Portal** — Card, ACH, wire, SEPA, BACS, AU Direct Debit. Subdomain or path routing.
- **Cash Application AI** — Auto-matches incoming payments to invoices by amount, reference, or customer.
- **Multi-Currency** — USD, GBP, AUD, CAD, EUR. KES, NGN, ZAR, INR on Scale.
