## Engagement playbook (between tweets)

- **T+0 to T+2h**: replies to PH comments, Hacker News replies, supporter DMs.
  Don't tweet — the launch IS the moment.
- **T+2h to T+4h**: skim Twitter for people tweeting about invoice chasing,
  slow payments, A/R pain. Quote-tweet their complaints with a "we built
  this for you" angle (Tweet 1 has the link; this is the soft-sell).
- **T+4h**: Tweet 2. The "why" message lands well when people are still
  talking about launches.
- **T+5h to T+8h**: do the press-outreach emails. If any blogger says "tell
  me more" — that's the moment for a demo.
- **T+8h**: Tweet 3. Numbers carry weight at end of day. Tag any early
  customers who gave permission to be quoted.

## What NOT to tweet

- Don't tweet before T+0 (Product Hunt penalizes coordinated pre-launch).
- Don't tweet the same link 3 times in a row. The link is in all 3 but
  the framing is different (announce, why, numbers).
- Don't argue with negative PH comments publicly. Reply once, kindly, then
  move on. The product speaks for itself.
