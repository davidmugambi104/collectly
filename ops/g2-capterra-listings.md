## Long description

Most A/R automation tools were built for 500+ person companies. The 5-50 person segment — the heart of the SMB economy — has been ignored. QuickBooks' built-in AR module is, frankly, unusable. HighRadius, Gaviti, and Growfin all cost $500-$3,000/month.

**Collectly is different.** We built AI-native A/R for the long tail.

**What you get:**
- **Tone-aware AI dunning** — friendly → firm → final sequences, written by GPT-4o, auto-pause on payment or reply
- **4-week cash-flow forecast** — based on aging and customer history, tells you when you can make payroll
- **Customer risk scoring** — predict which customers are most likely to pay late
- **Branded payment portal** — card, ACH, wire, and local rails (SEPA, BACS, AU Direct Debit)
- **Multi-currency** — USD, GBP, AUD, CAD, EUR on day one
- **QuickBooks + Xero + Stripe + Square + Plaid integrations** — 10-minute setup
- **Self-serve** — no sales call required

**Who it's for:**
- 5-50 person B2B service businesses
- $500K-$20M annual revenue
- US, UK, AU, CA, EU
- QuickBooks Online or Xero users
- Sells on net-30 or net-60 terms
- Founder/owner does AR today

**Who it's not for:**
- 1-2 person businesses (use Wave)
- 200+ person businesses (use HighRadius)
- Product businesses with no AR
- Construction with retainers (use Procore)

**Pricing:**

14-day free trial. No credit card. No per-invoice fees. No setup costs. No "premium" support tiers.
