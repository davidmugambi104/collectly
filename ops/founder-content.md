## Posting cadence

- X: 1-2 posts/day
- LinkedIn: 3-4 posts/week
- Substack: 1 essay/2 weeks
- Reddit: 1-2 posts/week, only in relevant subs, never spam

## Engagement rules

- Reply to every comment within 4 hours during business days
- Never delete critical comments (just respond thoughtfully)
- Never shill — be honest about what your tool does and doesn't do
- If you don't know, say so
- Always link to the blog post if you wrote a long-form version
