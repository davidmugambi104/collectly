## Target ICP (firmographic)

- US/UK/AU/CA B2B service businesses
- 5-50 employees
- $500K-$20M ARR
- Sells on net-30 or net-60 terms
- Uses QuickBooks Online or Xero
- Has 5+ open invoices at any time
- Founder/owner is the buyer

**Best-fit verticals:** design agencies, marketing agencies, management consulting, IT services, accounting/bookkeeping practices, law firms, software companies, freelance collectives, B2B SaaS early-stage.

## Buyer signals (use Apollo.io / LinkedIn Sales Nav / Store Leads)

- Title: Founder, Owner, CEO, COO, Head of Finance, Controller
- 2-50 employee company
- Mentions "AR" or "collections" in job posts
- Has accounts receivable roles
- Posts about cash flow, late payments, etc.
- Hiring AR or finance roles

## Reply handling

- **"How much does it cost?"** → Starter $49, Growth $99, Scale $199. Send pricing page.
- **"Does it work with [tool]?"** → Yes (QBO, Xero, Stripe, Square). For others, ask what they use and we'll add to the roadmap.
- **"I'm using [competitor]"** → Ask which, and why they're looking. Common: HighRadius too expensive, Gaviti not enough features, Growfin too enterprise.
- **"Send me a demo"** → Book a 15-min screen share, don't push.
- **"Not interested"** → Mark as closed-lost. Move on.
- **No reply after 4 emails** → Pause sequence for 90 days, then re-engage with a "checking back in" email.

## Volume target

- 50 cold emails/day = 250/week
- 5% reply rate = 12 replies/week
- 30% positive reply = 4 positive conversations/week
- 50% close = 2 new customers/week
- Avg $99/mo = $200 MRR added/week
- 100 weeks to $20K MRR
