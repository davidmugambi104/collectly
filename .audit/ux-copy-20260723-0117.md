## TRUST SIGNALS — REAL vs INVENTED

**🚨 Fake numbers on the homepage (page.tsx:80–84):**
- "Avg. days to payment: **12 days**"
- "Open invoices auto-handled: **94%**"
- "Avg. AR recovered / month: **$28.4K**"
- "Net Promoter Score: **74**"

There is no citation. The blog post `true-cost-of-late-payments` (line 22-26) cites QuickBooks 2025 data, but these specific stats on the homepage are not attributed. NPS 74 is a *very* high score — claiming it without a base size is a credibility risk.

**🚨 Fake customer names reused as logos AND testimonials:**
- Logo strip: "Acme Studios, Lumen & Co, Westgate Advisory, Brightline Legal, Harbor Painting, Northstar Marketing"
- Testimonial: "Sarah K., Founder, Lumen & Co"
- Customer page: "Lumen & Co, Westgate Advisory, Brightline Legal"
- Hero dashboard mock: same five names

It's internally consistent (good), but a sharp visitor will recognize they're fictional (e.g. the testimonial is attributed to "Sarah K." with last name redacted, and "47→14 days" claim reappears in the customer page). For a pre-launch/beta product, this is conventional — but **before public launch, replace at least 2-3 of these with real beta-customer testimonials (with permission) or label them clearly as "Sample data"** to avoid the FTC-relevance of "results not typical" for B2B testimonials.

**Real trust signals (good):**
- Founder name + signoff in blog posts
- "Built in Nairobi. Used globally." (geographic authenticity)
- Methodology citation on the ROI calculator
- Pricing page "What you will never pay for" anti-features (uncommon, very on-brand)
- Security page specifics (TLS, AES-256, KMS, Clerk RBAC)

## TOP UX FIXES — PRIORITY ORDER

### 🔴 P0 — Blocker before any public launch

1. **Replace the privacy policy placeholder** (`src/app/privacy/page.tsx:8`). "This is a placeholder. Replace with your real privacy policy before going live in production." Either write a real one or use a paid template (Termly, iubenda). Also do a quick pass on `/terms` and `/dpa` (not inspected in detail — verify they're real, not skeletons).
2. **Reconcile the "free beta" messaging with the "Start free" CTAs.** Either the site is in private beta (then the CTAs should say "Request access" or "Join the waitlist") or it's public (then remove the "all plans are free right now" line). Right now it reads as two different products on two different pages.
3. **Add a visible primary CTA to the home hero.** The hero currently has an unlabeled waitlist form where the submit button text isn't visible until interaction. Either give it a "Get early access" button label, or replace the form with a "Start free" button → `/sign-up`.

### 🟡 P1 — Fix before paid launch

4. **Cite the homepage stats** (page.tsx:80-84) or remove them. "NPS 74" is a very bold claim without a denominator. Either label them "Target metrics for our design partners" or replace with real data once you have it.
5. **Decide on the "fake customer" naming.** Either (a) replace the 3 testimonials + 6 logos with real beta customers, or (b) label them clearly as "Demo data" / "Sample customer names" in a small footnote. Current state: a sharp visitor can tell, and that's a credibility hit.
6. **Add a skip-to-content link** to both `<MarketingHeader />` and `<AppShell />` (1 line of HTML each, big a11y win).
7. **Wrap the marketing pages in `<main>`** (or use a `<main>` in `<MarketingHeader>`'s child layout). Currently no page has a `<main>` landmark except `/tools/ar-roi` and a couple of dashboard pages. SEO + a11y.
8. **Audit `text-ink-400` / `text-ink-500` on white** — likely below WCAG AA on small text. Worst offenders: footer "Built in Nairobi. Used globally." copy, hero stat tile secondary labels, dashboard KPI sub-labels. Either darken to `text-ink-600` or increase font weight.
9. **Remove "David"** from `dashboard/billing/page.tsx:258` — replace with "the team" or "the founder" for professionalism, or leave as-is if the personal-touch is the brand (it's defensible for a beta).

### 🟢 P2 — Polish, do whenever

10. **Standardize the changelog nav.** Either give it `<MarketingHeader />` (with maybe a "Back home" replacement) or document the exception in a comment.
11. **Tighten the mobile menu focus trap.** When the drawer opens, focus the first link; on close, return focus to the hamburger. ~15 lines of code.
12. **A/B test the header layout.** The "Get $25 for an interview" link is wedged between two other links in the middle of the nav. Move it to the end as a button-style chip, or to the top-right next to "Start free", so it doesn't get lost.
13. **Replace the fake browser-chrome URL** "app.getcollectly.app/dashboard" with something shorter or `truncate` it — only matters on very narrow phones.
14. **Add a real form-error state** to the waitlist form (currently uses HTML `required` only, so the UX is browser-default). Same for `/playbook`, `/interview`, and `/contact` waitlist embed.
15. **Pricing page: show the Enterprise tier** in the grid (or at least a 4th "Enterprise — Talk to sales" card) so visitors don't have to hunt. You already have it in `PLAN_PRICING.enterprise` (utils.ts:53) — it's a one-liner to add.

## SUMMARY

**Site is in good shape for a beta.** Copy is on-brand, consistent, and almost free of placeholder text. The one real placeholder (privacy) is a P0 blocker. The home-page stats and the fake customer names are the next-biggest credibility risks. Navigation is consistent across all marketing pages, the 404 and error pages are clean, the dashboard has a real loading skeleton, and a11y basics (lang attribute, aria-labels on icon buttons, semantic headings) are mostly there.

**Quick wins (≤1 day):** add a real privacy policy, label the home stats as illustrative, give the home hero CTA a visible button, add `<main>` + skip-link to the marketing shell.

**Big wins (≤1 week):** swap in real beta testimonials, do a color-contrast sweep on `text-ink-400`/`500`.
