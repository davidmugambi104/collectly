## 1. Accounting Firm Partnerships

### How SaaS Tools Partner with Accounting Firms

Accounting software referral programs are uniquely powerful because accountants and bookkeepers are **high-leverage referral partners** — a single accountant managing dozens of client accounts can refer businesses repeatedly. Industry benchmarks:

- **Average referral rate:** 12–22% of accountant partners actively refer clients (vs. 5–10% for end-user referrals)
- **Conversion rate:** 30–50% of accountant-referred leads convert to paid customers (vs. 15–25% for peer referrals)
- **Market size:** Cloud accounting market expected to surpass $20B by 2026

### Proven Partnership Models (from QuickBooks, Xero, Sage, FreshBooks, Bench)

| Company | Model | Incentive | Key Mechanism |
|---------|-------|-----------|---------------|
| **QuickBooks** | ProAdvisor Program (250K+ globally) | Revenue share + wholesale pricing + referral bonuses | Certified accountants recommend QBO to clients; tiered benefits |
| **Xero** | Partner Program (Bronze→Platinum tiers) | Partner revenue share, wholesale pricing, tiered benefits | Partner directory listing brings leads TO partners (mutual benefit) |
| **Sage** | Partner Program | Tiered commissions, co-marketing support, referral bonuses | Multi-product portfolio enables cross-selling |
| **FreshBooks** | Referral + Affiliate | Commission when referrals subscribe; referred users get extended trials | Dashboard-based referral link sharing |
| **Bench** | Partner Program for Accountants + Referral | $150 per new client; referred clients get first-month discount | Offloads bookkeeping from accountants; partner discount + referral rewards |

### What Would Incentivize a Bookkeeping Firm to Recommend an AR Tool?

1. **Revenue share (recurring):** 15–30% recurring commission aligns long-term incentives. Accountants have ongoing client relationships, so recurring commissions compound.
2. **Wholesale/partner pricing:** Discounted pricing for partner-managed accounts makes the accountant look good (they pass savings to clients).
3. **Co-marketing:** Pre-written email campaigns, co-branded landing pages, partner directory listings (like Xero's advisor directory) — these bring leads TO the accounting firm.
4. **White-label option:** Some firms want to offer AR automation under their own brand as part of a service bundle. This is especially attractive for larger bookkeeping firms.
5. **Client retention lift:** AR tools that reduce client cash flow problems make the accountant look good and reduce client churn. This is the *non-financial* incentive — making their clients stickier.
6. **Practice efficiency:** If the AR tool reduces the accountant's own workload (less manual chasing, better reconciliation data), that's a direct benefit regardless of commission.

### Recommended Structure for Collectly

- **Tier 1 (Referral Partner):** 20% recurring commission, co-branded landing page, partner directory listing
- **Tier 2 (Certified Partner, 5+ referrals/yr):** 25% recurring commission, wholesale pricing for partner-managed accounts, access to Partner Central (marketing templates, guides), co-branded referral landing page
- **Tier 3 (Strategic Partner, 10+ referrals/yr):** 30% recurring commission, white-label option, dedicated partner manager, co-marketing campaigns

## 2. QBO ProAdvisor Program

### Scale & Influence

- **250,000+ ProAdvisors globally** — the largest network of professional accounting and small business advisors specializing in QuickBooks
- Program is 25+ years old, recently rebranded as **Intuit ProPartner** (evolving from ProAdvisor)
- ProAdvisors are "key influencers in the accounting industry" and "expert guides for small business owners" (Intuit's own language)
- Program is free to join; tiers based on certifications and active client subscriptions

### How ProAdvisors Discover & Recommend Apps

1. **QuickBooks App Store:** Apps listed on the QBO App Store appear in the "Apps" tab in QuickBooks Online Accountant. ProAdvisors browse apps by category and recommend them to clients.
2. **"Accountant ready" flag:** When listing an app on the QBO App Store, developers can enable the **Accountant ready** option so the app appears in the ProAdvisor's QBOA interface.
3. **Intuit's "Find an Accountant" page:** Developers can search for ProAdvisors by industry, location, and business type, then reach out directly with special pricing or training offers.
4. **ProAdvisor Academy:** Intuit offers training on third-party app integrations as part of ProAdvisor certifications.
5. **Firm of the Future community:** Intuit's content hub for accountants, where apps can get featured.

### Can You Sponsor or Get Featured?

- **App Store listing:** Free for approved apps in the Intuit App Partner Program (available in US, UK, Australia, Canada).
- **Intuit App Partner Program:** Requires approval process. Once approved, you get listed in the App Store and can market to ProAdvisors.
- **Direct outreach:** Intuit explicitly encourages app developers to contact ProAdvisors directly via the Find an Accountant directory, offering special pricing or training.
- **Intuit events:** Sponsorship opportunities at Intuit events (QuickBooks Connect, scaling events).
- **Accountant-approved app recommendations:** Intuit's program includes "vertical specific app recommendations to improve client efficiency" — getting featured here is high-value.

### Influence on SMB Tool Purchasing

ProAdvisors have **enormous influence** on SMB tool purchasing. Millions of SMBs rely on ProAdvisors for accounting advice and training. A recommendation from a ProAdvisor is effectively a trusted advisor endorsement — conversion rates from accountant referrals (30–50%) dwarf organic conversion rates. For an AR tool, ProAdvisors are the single most important channel because:
- They see client cash flow problems firsthand
- They're already recommending accounting-adjacent tools
- They manage multiple client accounts (each = a potential Collectly customer)

### Key Action Items for Collectly

1. **Apply for Intuit App Partner Program** (if not already done)
2. **Enable "Accountant ready" flag** on QBO App Store listing
3. **Build a ProAdvisor landing page** with special pricing for ProAdvisors and their clients
4. **Direct outreach** via Find an Accountant directory — target ProAdvisors who serve industries with high AR complexity (professional services, construction, B2B services)
5. **Create ProAdvisor training materials** (quick demo videos, setup guides)
6. **Pursue ProAdvisor Academy inclusion** — get Collectly featured in Intuit's app training curriculum

### Stripe Partner Ecosystem

Stripe has a well-developed partner ecosystem with multiple tracks:

- **Services track:** For agencies/consultants who implement Stripe
- **SaaS Platforms track:** For software companies embedding Stripe — includes co-marketing, co-selling, partner directory listing
- **Apps track:** Build apps that appear in Stripe's product flows (Stripe App Marketplace)
- **Payment Methods track:** Launch payment methods on Stripe

**Relevance to Collectly:** If Collectly integrates with Stripe (e.g., for payment processing, automatic invoice payment, payment reminders with Stripe payment links), listing in the **Stripe App Marketplace** provides discovery. Stripe has 2,300+ partners. The Kajabi case study shows accounting integrations via Stripe Connect are achievable in weeks.

**Key benefit:** Co-marketing resources, partner directory listing, partner marks/badges, early access to product previews.

- Shopify has 3,300+ partners and a massive app ecosystem

- **Relevance:** E-commerce businesses with B2B invoices (wholesale, net-terms) need AR automation. Collectly could list as a Shopify app for merchants who offer net-terms invoicing.

- **Relevance:** WooCommerce merchants doing B2B sales need invoice chasing and payment reminders. A WooCommerce extension for Collectly could open up the WordPress/SMB e-commerce segment.

### Square

## 5. Reseller / Agency Channel

### Could Agencies Resell or Implement Collectly?

**Yes — this is a proven model in SaaS.** Key examples:

| Program | Commission | Partner Type | Model |
|---------|-----------|-------------|-------|
| HubSpot Solutions Partner | Up to 20% recurring | Marketing/CRM agencies | Resell + implement + certify |
| Zendesk Partner | 10–20% recurring | IT consultants, BPOs | Resell + implement, tiered (Qualified→Advanced→Premier) |
| Monday.com Partner | 20–30% margin | Workflow consultants | Tiered with dedicated account managers at higher tiers |
| Text Partner Program | 20–25% revenue share | Agencies, BPOs, consultants | Choice of referral OR reseller model |
| ActiveCampaign Agency | Wholesale discount → resell at markup | Marketing automation agencies | Buy wholesale, resell at markup, OR standard commission |
| GoHighLevel | 40% lifetime recurring + 5% second-tier | Marketing agencies | Full white-label, $97–$297/mo subscription tiers |
| Vendasta | 20–60% margin | Local marketing agencies | White-label marketplace of 250+ apps |

### What the Agency Reseller Model Looks Like for Collectly

**Target agencies:**
- Accounting consulting firms (not just bookkeepers — firms that advise on financial operations)
- Digital transformation agencies (helping SMBs modernize finance operations)
- IT/MSP providers serving SMBs (bundling finance tools with IT services)
- Boutique finance/ops consultants

**Model options:**

1. **Referral (lightest touch):** Agency refers clients, earns 20% recurring commission. Collectly handles all billing, onboarding, support.
2. **Reseller (medium touch):** Agency buys Collectly at wholesale (30–40% discount), resells to clients at their own price, handles first-line support. Collectly provides training and technical support to the agency.
3. **Implementation Partner (service-heavy):** Agency sells implementation services (setup, template configuration, workflow design, training) and includes Collectly subscription as part of a package. Agency earns implementation fees + commission on subscription.
4. **White-label (deepest):** Agency brands Collectly under their own name, bundles with their existing services. Higher commitment, higher revenue share (30–40%).

**Key success factors:**
- Partner portal with training, demo accounts, marketing materials
- Deal registration system to prevent channel conflict
- Tiered structure (more referrals → higher commission + more support)
- Certification program (Collectly Certified Partner)
- Co-marketing support (joint webinars, case studies, blog posts)

## 6. Affiliate Programs in B2B SaaS

### Typical Commission Structures

| Range | Structure | Best For |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| **5–30%** | Percentage of sale value | General SaaS affiliate |
| **15–25%** | Recurring (most common for mature programs) | Subscription SaaS |
| **20–30%** | Recurring (baseline for content-driven affiliates) | Programs that successfully recruit content affiliates |
| **$200–$1,000** | Flat-fee CPA | High-ticket/enterprise SaaS |
| **30–50%** | Lifetime recurring | Top-tier programs (GoHighLevel at 40%, ClickFunnels at 40%) |

### Industry Benchmarks

- **SaaS affiliate commission landscape:** Typically 5–30% of sale value; mature programs settle at 15–25%
- **AI/ML SaaS:** ~24.5% average commission rate
- **Creator economy tools:** 12–22% MRR contribution through affiliates
- **Cookie duration:** 90 days minimum recommended for B2B SaaS (120 days for enterprise/high-consideration)
- **Average B2B SaaS sales cycle:** 4–8 weeks — standard 30-day cookies drop attribution for many conversions
- **Affiliate activation rate:** <10% industry average (affiliates generating ≥1 referral within 90 days). Best-in-class programs achieve 50%+ with structured onboarding.

### Does Affiliate Work for AR Tools Specifically?

**Yes, but with caveats:**

1. **Consultants and agencies are the gold standard** for B2B SaaS affiliates. They already recommend tools to clients and have immense trust. A finance consultant who recommends Collectly to 10 clients/month is worth more than 100 coupon sites.
2. **Content affiliates** (comparison sites, "best AR tools" articles, G2/Capterra reviewers, YouTube "vs" video creators) drive top-of-funnel discovery. Getting Collectly reviewed on G2, Capterra, and comparison blogs is critical.
3. **Coupon sites often cannibalize** existing sales rather than generating incremental revenue. Exclude or de-prioritize coupon affiliates.
4. **Niche newsletters** read by CFOs, controllers, and finance managers are high-value affiliate channels.
5. **Community-embedded practitioners** (Slack groups, Reddit r/accounting, LinkedIn communities) provide recommendations that paid ads cannot replicate.

### Recommended Collectly Affiliate Structure

- **20% recurring commission** (lifetime, for content affiliates and consultants)
- **25% recurring commission** for certified partners/agencies (Tier 2)
- **$150 flat bounty** option for one-time referrers (accountants who don't want to manage ongoing affiliate relationships)
- **90-day cookie duration** (B2B sales cycles are long)
- **No coupon sites** in the program (avoid cannibalization)
- **Structured 30-day onboarding** for new affiliates (clear first-commission milestone, ready-to-deploy promotional assets, direct communication channel)

## 7. Competitor Partnership Strategies

### Chaser (Direct Competitor — AR Automation)

**Partner Program Structure:**
- **Referral Partner Program** targeted at accountants and bookkeepers
- **Incentives:**
  - £25 voucher when a referred client signs up for a trial
  - £200 voucher when the client subscribes to Chaser
  - No limit on referrals
- **Tiered benefits based on volume:**

| Referrals/Year | <5 | 5–9 | 10+ |
|----------------|-----|-----|-----|
| Monetary | £25 trial + £200 per subscription | Same | Same |
| Marketing support | — | Partner Central access + partner directory listing | Partner Central + co-branded referral landing page + directory listing |

- **Partner Central:** Marketing templates and guides, free of charge
- **Partner pricing:** Available with details at help.chaserhq.com
- **Distribution:** Xero advisor directory, QBO App Store, partner directory on chaserhq.com
- **Pricing:** £199–£899/month (Compact to Complete tiers)

**Key Takeaways from Chaser:**
- Voucher-based model (not recurring commission) — simpler but less sticky for partners
- Low barrier to entry (just sign up for a referral link)
- Partner Central with marketing templates is table stakes
- Co-branded landing page only at 10+ referrals/year (tiered reward)
- Strong Xero integration (UK-focused, where Xero is strong)

### Satago (AR + Invoice Finance)

**Partner Program:**
- Targets accountants and finance brokers
- Offers: Invoice finance tools + AR automation in a single platform
- Partner benefits: Referral commissions, branded portals, risk dashboards, marketing kits
- Distribution: Sage Advice referral (satago.com/?referralCode=sage-advice), Xero marketplace
- **Key differentiator:** Combines AR automation with invoice financing — partners can offer funding solutions alongside collections automation

**Key Takeaways from Satago:**
- Bundling AR + financing makes the partner offering more compelling (cash flow solution, not just collections)
- Branded portals and risk dashboards give partners ongoing utility (not just a referral link)
- Marketing kits provided to partners (similar to Chaser's Partner Central)

### Fundbox (Invoice Financing + AR)

**Partner Program:**
- Full-stack capital platform for embedded lending
- Partners include FreshBooks, Nav, SoFi
- **Partner benefits:**
  - Credit decisioning infrastructure
  - Institutional capital
  - Customized white-labeled UX (API-driven embedded experiences)
  - Bank-grade compliance
  - Go-to-market playbooks + hands-on marketing/sales support
  - End-to-end servicing (money movement, customer support)
- **Results:** $7B+ total capital funded, <1 min median underwriting time, <2 weeks to onboard platforms

**Key Takeaways from Fundbox:**
- Fundbox has pivoted from direct SMB lending to **embedded capital platform** — they partner with accounting/invoicing platforms to embed financing
- GTM playbooks and hands-on marketing support = high-touch partner enablement
- White-label/API-first approach enables deep integration partnerships
- The combination of "AR data + financing" is powerful — Collectly could partner with a lending platform to offer financing against outstanding invoices

### Phase 1 (Immediate — 0–3 months)

1. **Launch a Partner Program** with a simple landing page and referral link system
   - 20% recurring commission for all partners
   - $150 bounty option for accountants who prefer one-time payments
   - Partner portal with marketing templates and demo videos
2. **Get listed on QBO App Store** with "Accountant ready" flag enabled
3. **Direct outreach to 50 ProAdvisors** via Intuit's Find an Accountant directory — offer free trials and special partner pricing
4. **Get listed on Xero App Marketplace** (if not already) and pursue Xero Partner Program integration
5. **List on G2 and Capterra** — build reviews to enable content affiliate discovery

### Phase 2 (Near-term — 3–6 months)

6. **Build Partner Central** — a self-serve portal with:
   - Marketing templates (email campaigns, social posts, one-pagers)
   - Demo videos and product walkthroughs
   - Co-branded landing page builder
   - Deal registration system
   - Commission tracking dashboard
7. **Tier the partner program** (Referral → Certified → Strategic)
8. **Apply for Stripe App Marketplace** listing (if Stripe integration exists)
9. **Recruit 10–25 hand-selected affiliates** — target accounting bloggers, newsletter operators, and finance consultants
10. **Launch partner directory** on collectly.com (like Xero's advisor directory)

### Phase 3 (Medium-term — 6–12 months)

11. **Pursue Shopify App Store listing** for B2B/wholesale merchants
12. **Build agency reseller channel** — target 5–10 accounting consulting firms as implementation partners
13. **Partner with fractional CFO networks** (CFO Centre, Paro) — offer Collectly as a recommended tool in their client engagements
14. **White-label option** for Tier 3 partners
15. **Co-marketing campaigns** with top-performing partners (webinars, case studies, joint blog posts)
16. **Consider Gusto/cross-referral partnership** for SMB channel

### Key Metrics to Track

- Partner-sourced pipeline (% of total pipeline)
- Partner-sourced revenue (% of new MRR)
- Time-to-first-deal for new partners
- Partner activation rate (target: >25% within 90 days)
- Average partner lifetime value
- Channel conflict incidents (should be ~0 with deal registration)
- Partner NPS
