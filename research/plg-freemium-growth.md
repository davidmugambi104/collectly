### Key Patterns

1. **Enterprise AR = Sales-led, demo-only, quote-based pricing.** Billtrust, Versapay, Growfin, Tesorio, HighRadius, Esker, Quadient AR, and Gaviti all require demos. Implementation takes 2–12 weeks. No freemium, no self-serve signup. This is the dominant model for mid-market+ AR.

2. **SMB AR = Product-led or hybrid, with free trials or free tiers.** Chaser, Satago, Paidnice, Merclex, Upflow, and Bill.com all offer some form of self-serve entry point — free trial, free tier, or low-cost flat pricing with instant signup.

3. **Accounting ecosystem integrations are the primary distribution channel for SMB AR tools.** Chaser built around Xero/QuickBooks. Paidnice won 2025 Xero Global Small Business App of the Year. The Xero and QuickBooks app marketplaces are major acquisition channels.

4. **Partner/accountant channel programs are widespread.** Chaser has a Partner Program for accountants. Bill.com has a VAR Partner Program with recurring revenue share. Invoiced (now Flywire) has a formal Partner Program. Forwardly has a Certified Partner program with referral rewards. Nickel has an accounting firm partner program with revenue share.

5. **No pure PLG success story in AR yet.** Unlike dev tools (Stripe) or spend management (Ramp), no AR automation company has achieved breakout growth through pure product-led motion. The category remains dominated by sales-led GTM for serious revenue.

6. **Emerging freemium entrants.** Merclex (2025) launched with a free starter tier covering credit screening + monitoring + collections — the first true freemium AR tool. Upflow offers a free analytics tier as a wedge. Satago has long had a limited free plan.

## 2. PLG in B2B Fintech — What Works and What Fails

### PLG Success Stories in Adjacent Fintech

| Company | PLG Motion | Key Activation Signal | Why PLG Worked |
|---------|-----------|----------------------|----------------|
| **Stripe** | Developer-first API, self-serve signup | First successful API call / first payment processed | Developer audience; low friction; instant value; no compliance gate for sandbox |
| **Ramp** | Self-serve signup, instant card issuance | First card transaction / first savings identified | Clear ROI (cost savings); fast onboarding; no KYC friction for initial signup |
| **Brex** | Self-serve signup for VC-backed startups | Card activation + first expense | Targeted a specific niche (VC-backed startups) underserved by traditional banks |
| **Wave** | Free accounting + invoicing for freelancers | First invoice sent | SMB/freelancer audience; simple product; viral through invoice recipient network |
| **Plaid** | Developer-first API, sandbox access | First successful data connection | Developer audience; sandbox removes all friction |

### What Makes PLG Work in B2B Fintech

1. **Developer audience or individual operator buyer.** PLG works when the end user can evaluate and adopt the product without a committee. Stripe/Plaid work because developers test APIs. Ramp works because finance teams can see savings immediately.

2. **Time-to-value under 5 minutes.** Elite PLG companies (per Prospeo analysis of 446 B2B SaaS companies) target <5 min TTV. For fintech, this means: sandbox access, instant card issuance, or first invoice sent within minutes.

3. **Trust must be established before PLG works.** Critical insight from the 2026 B2B Fintech Marketing Playbook: "PLG works in fintech only after you've established brand trust. Stripe's self-serve onboarding works because 10 years of brand-building made them trusted." For AR tools, this means compliance signals (SOC 2, security badges), social proof (G2 reviews, case studies), and transparent pricing are prerequisites.

4. **Hybrid PLG + Enterprise Sales is the winning model.** Per Vantage Point's 2026 analysis: "Fintechs using hybrid PLG + enterprise sales motions see 40%+ PQL-to-SQL conversion rates and 50%+ product-sourced pipeline." Brex's analysis found every one of the 50 fastest-growing software vendors runs a product-led motion. The pattern: let self-serve users experience value, then layer sales for enterprise expansion.

5. **Regulatory/compliance friction kills pure PLG.** KYC/AML requirements, enterprise procurement cycles, and data sensitivity concerns add friction that pure PLG can't absorb. AR tools that touch payment processing face these constraints.

### What Makes PLG Fail in B2B Fintech

1. **Treating fintech buyers like SaaS buyers.** "Fintech buyers reject aggressive growth tactics (viral loops, friction-light signups, '100% free!') that work for SaaS. They want institutional feel." (Toolradar, 2026)

2. **No trust investment before PLG push.** Launching freemium without established brand trust = low conversion and potentially reputational damage.

3. **Ignoring compliance in onboarding.** KYC/AML gates must be built into the flow, not bolted on. Block sales engagement for unverified accounts.

4. **Over-indexing on paid search.** Fintech CPCs are $50–$200. Paid search works for branded defense only. Scale via content SEO, newsletters, and podcasts instead.

### Key Activation Metrics for B2B Fintech PLG

| Metric | Description | Weight as PQL Signal |
|--------|-------------|---------------------|
| API calls / transaction volume | Core product usage intensity | Very High |
| Features activated | Number of core features used | High |
| Team members invited | Multi-user adoption | Very High |
| Integrations connected | Third-party systems linked (ERPs, banks, accounting) | High |
| Compliance docs requested | SOC 2 downloads, security questionnaires | Medium |
| Login frequency | DAU/WAU ratio | Medium |

## 3. Freemium vs Free Trial vs Demo-Only — What Works for AR Tools

### 2026 B2B SaaS Conversion Benchmarks (ChartMogul / Growth Unhinged / ProductLed)

| Freemium (ungated, no account) | Emerging | ~7–9% | 7–9% | 8–12% |

### Per-1,000-Visitors Funnel (ChartMogul 2026)

| Model | Signups | Paying Customers |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| Freemium (standard) | 90 | ~5 |
| Free trial (no CC) | 45 | ~3.6 |
| Freemium (ungated) | 70 | ~5.6 |
| Free trial (CC required) | 35 | ~10.5 |

**Key insight:** Freemium produces more paying customers per 1,000 visitors than standard free trials (5 vs 3.6), despite lower per-signup conversion. Credit-card-required trials produce the most paying customers (10.5) but the fewest signups.

### What Works Best for AR Tools Specifically

**Recommendation: Free trial (no CC) or reverse trial for SMB segment; demo-only for enterprise.**

Reasoning:
- **AR tools require integration setup.** Users need to connect QuickBooks/Xero/NetSuite before experiencing value. A 14-day free trial gives enough time for this. Freemium's permanent free tier is less natural because the integration setup is a one-time event, not an ongoing exploration.
- **ERP integration complexity makes freemium expensive to support.** Free users connecting to NetSuite or SAP consume support resources. SMB tools (Chaser, Paidnice) that integrate with simpler Xero/QBO can support freemium more easily.
- **The emerging freemium wedge for AR is analytics/visibility, not automation.** Upflow's free analytics tier (track DSO and aging before paying) is a smart PLG wedge. Users get value from seeing their AR metrics, then upgrade for automation. This pattern works because the free tier is lightweight (no integrations needed beyond accounting sync) while the paid tier delivers the heavy ROI.
- **Demo-only remains the standard for enterprise AR.** Billtrust, Versapay, Growfin, Tesorio all use demo-only. This won't change — enterprise AR buyers need custom scoping, security review, and implementation planning.

## 4. Self-Serve Onboarding for AR Tools

### What Good Self-Serve AR Onboarding Looks Like

**The minimum viable onboarding flow (target: <10 minutes to first value):**

1. **Signup** (email + password, or Google/Microsoft SSO) — 30 seconds
2. **Company info** (name, industry, size) — 30 seconds
3. **Connect accounting system** (Xero/QBO OAuth, 1-click) — 2 minutes
4. **Auto-import receivables** (invoices, customers, balances sync automatically) — 1 minute
5. **Show AR dashboard** (DSO, aging buckets, overdue invoices — instantly populated from sync) — **THIS IS THE AHA MOMENT**
6. **Guide first action** (send first automated reminder to a test customer, or view a recommended collections task) — 2 minutes
7. **Offer upgrade / book demo** (contextual, based on data seen) — 1 minute

### How to Reduce Time-to-Value

1. **OAuth-based accounting integration.** No CSV imports, no manual data entry. One-click connect to Xero/QBO via OAuth. This is table stakes — Chaser, Upflow, Paidnice, and Merclex all do this.

2. **Pre-populated dashboards from sync data.** The moment accounting data syncs, show the user their DSO, aging buckets, and at-risk invoices. This is the "aha" — they see their AR situation visualized instantly without any manual work.

3. **Templated reminder sequences.** Don't make users design email templates from scratch. Pre-built, industry-specific reminder sequences that they can customize later. Chaser does this well with their "human-sounding" reminder templates.

4. **Progressive disclosure.** Show the dashboard first. Reveal automation features, workflow builders, and advanced analytics after the user has seen their data. Don't overwhelm with a 50-feature product tour on day one.

5. **In-app onboarding checklist.** A simple 4–5 step checklist:
   - ✅ Connect your accounting system
   - ✅ Review your AR dashboard
   - ✅ Set up your first reminder sequence
   - ✅ Send a test reminder
   - ✅ Invite a team member

6. **Accountant/collaborator invite as an activation step.** Inviting an accountant or team member is both an activation milestone and a virality mechanism. Prompt this early in onboarding.

| Tool | Time to Value | Approach |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Stuut | 3–4 days | Autonomous agent |
| Chaser | Days to 1 week | Workflow automation, Xero/QBO ecosystem |

**For a PLG AR tool targeting SMB, target time-to-first-value should be under 10 minutes** (dashboard populated) and time-to-first-action (first reminder sent) under 30 minutes.

## 5. In-Product Virality for AR Tools

### Natural Virality Mechanisms in AR/Invoicing

AR tools have unique virality advantages that most B2B SaaS categories lack:

#### A. Invoice Recipient Network Effects
Every invoice sent through an AR tool reaches a customer (the invoice recipient). That recipient interacts with the tool's payment portal, invoice viewer, or payment link. This creates natural exposure to non-users.

- **Wave** built an entire business on this: free invoicing for freelancers → invoice recipients see Wave branding → some sign up for Wave themselves.
- **Versapay** reports 5M+ companies transacting through its network. Each new supplier brings their buyers into the network, reducing friction for future transactions.
- **Strategy:** Include a subtle "Powered by [Collectly]" on invoice payment pages. Make the payment portal so good that invoice recipients want to use it for their own AR.

#### B. Accountant / Bookkeeper Channel
Accountants manage AR for multiple clients. If an accountant adopts an AR tool for one client, they naturally recommend it to others.

- **Chaser Partner Program:** Accountants become partners, earn revenue share, and deploy Chaser across their client base.
- **Bill.com VAR Program:** ERP value-added resellers earn recurring revenue share for every customer referred. Partners include Armanino, Baker Tilly, Wipfli.
- **Forwardly Certified Partner:** Accountants get certified, earn referral rewards ($100–$115 per referred business), and get public badges for LinkedIn/website.
- **Strategy:** Build an accountant-first partner program with: revenue share (10–20%), multi-client dashboard (manage all clients from one login), certification program, and co-marketing resources.

#### C. Team Invites
AR involves multiple stakeholders: finance managers, controllers, AR clerks, sales (for collections escalation), and customer success (for at-risk accounts). Each invite expands the user base within an account.

- **Activation signal:** "Team members invited" is the highest-weighted PQL signal in B2B fintech (per Vantage Point 2026).
- **Strategy:** Make team invites frictionless. Pre-populate role-based permissions. Suggest inviting an accountant or CFO during onboarding. Send the invitee a branded email that shows them their client's AR dashboard (read-only view).

#### D. Referral Programs
Direct referral programs in the AR/invoicing space:

| Company | Referral Offer | Structure |
|---------|---------------|-----------|
| Invoice Ninja | 50% commission for 3 years | Open to all users, no cap |
| Bill.com | Recurring revenue share | VAR/accountant partners only |
| Forwardly | $100–$115 per referred business | Certified partner program |
| Invoiced (Flywire) | Partner program with training | Accounting firms |
| Chaser | Partner program (revenue share) | Accountants |

**Strategy for Collectly:** Offer a dual referral program:
1. **User referral:** Current users get 1 month free for every successful referral that converts to paid. Simple, everyone can participate.
2. **Accountant partner program:** Revenue share (15–20% recurring), multi-client dashboard, certification, and co-marketing. This is the higher-leverage channel.

#### E. Collaborative Features as Virality Drivers
- **Shared invoice approval workflows:** When a CFO needs to approve an invoice before it's sent, they're brought into the product.
- **Customer payment portal:** Invoice recipients log in to view, dispute, and pay invoices — creating accounts and exposure.
- **Collections escalation to sales/CS:** When an invoice is overdue, the AR tool can route the case to the account owner (sales/CS), bringing them into the product.
- **Shared aging reports:** Exportable/shareable AR reports that finance teams send to leadership, creating organic awareness.

### AR-Specific North Star Metrics

**Primary North Star Metric candidates for an AR tool:**

1. **Weekly active accounts with 3+ automated reminder sequences running** — captures real automation adoption, not just dashboard viewing
2. **Outstanding invoice dollars under automated management** — captures the scale of AR being actively worked by the tool
3. **DSO reduction achieved (days)** — the ultimate value metric, but lagging
4. **Invoices paid through the platform per week** — captures transactional engagement and payment portal usage

**Recommended North Star for Collectly:** *Weekly active accounts with $50K+ outstanding under automated management*

**Supporting metrics:**
- **Activation rate:** % of signups who connect an accounting system + enable at least one automated reminder sequence within 24 hours
- **Time-to-first-reminder:** Minutes from signup to first automated reminder sent (target: <30 min)
- **DSO reduction:** Days reduced from baseline within 60 days of activation
- **Team activation rate:** % of seats within an account that are actively using the tool (target: >60% within 30 days)
- **Invoice recipient engagement:** % of invoices sent through the platform that are viewed via the payment portal (virality indicator)
- **Accountant partner-sourced revenue:** % of new revenue from accountant referral channel (target: >30% at scale)

## 7. Strategic Recommendations for Collectly

### Go-To-Market Model: Hybrid PLG + Sales-Assisted

1. **Lead with a free tier (analytics) + free trial (automation).** Offer a free AR analytics dashboard (DSO, aging, overdue tracking) that requires only an accounting integration. Upsell automation features (reminder sequences, payment portal, collections CRM) via a 14-day free trial.

2. **Self-serve onboarding under 10 minutes.** OAuth-based Xero/QBO integration → auto-populated dashboard → guided first action. No demo required for SMB segment.

3. **Layer sales for mid-market expansion.** Use PQL scoring (invoice volume, team invites, integrations connected, multi-entity signals) to trigger sales outreach for accounts showing enterprise readiness.

4. **Build an accountant partner program from day one.** This is the highest-leverage distribution channel in AR. Offer 15–20% recurring revenue share, multi-client dashboard, certification, and co-marketing.

5. **Leverage invoice recipient virality.** Every invoice sent through Collectly should include a "Powered by Collectly" payment portal link. Make the payment experience so good that recipients want to use it themselves.

6. **Content + SEO as primary acquisition channel.** Fintech CPCs are $50–$200, making paid search unsustainable. Invest in AR/collections content (DSO benchmarks, dunning best practices, invoice template library), Xero/QBO app marketplace presence, and finance newsletter sponsorships.

7. **Target the Xero/QuickBooks ecosystem first.** These ecosystems have the most SMB AR pain, the easiest integrations, and established app marketplaces. Expand to NetSuite/Sage Intacct for mid-market.

### Pricing Architecture Suggestion

| Tier | Price | Target | Key Features |
|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| Free | $0 | SMB / individual | AR dashboard, DSO tracking, aging report, basic invoice viewer, 1 accounting integration |
| Starter | $49–$99/mo | Small business | Automated email reminders, reminder templates, payment portal, up to 500 invoices/mo |
| Growth | $199–$399/mo | Mid-market | Multi-user, collections CRM, workflows, SMS reminders, custom templates, up to 5,000 invoices/mo |
| Enterprise | Quote-based | Large mid-market / Enterprise | Multi-entity, ERP integration (NetSuite/Sage), cash application, advanced analytics, dedicated CSM |

### Key Risks to Watch

1. **Free tier support costs.** SMB users on free plans connecting to Xero/QBO will generate support tickets. Limit free tier to dashboard only (no automation) to keep costs manageable.
2. **PLG plateau.** Pure PLG companies hit a revenue plateau around $10–20M ARR when self-serve users resist enterprise upgrades. Build the sales motion early.
3. **Trust deficit.** AR is financially sensitive. Without SOC 2, security badges, and public case studies, PLG conversion will suffer. Invest in trust signals before scaling freemium.
4. **Competitive response.** Merclex (2025) already launched with a free tier. Chaser and Paidnice have established positions in Xero/QBO ecosystem. Differentiate on onboarding speed, automation depth, and accountant channel.
