# Warmup Email Templates

Use these for the 14-day warmup. No pitches. No links. No product mentions.

## Goal of every warmup email
Get a reply. Replies are the signal that builds sender reputation. Make it easy and natural for the recipient to answer.

---

## Template 1 — Pure catch-up
```
Subject: Quick catch up

Hey [Name],

Hope you're doing well. What's keeping you busy these days?

Reply and let me know — always curious what you're working on.

Davie
```

## Template 2 — Specific question
```
Subject: How's [company/project]?

Hi [Name],

Saw [specific thing] and thought of you. How's [company/project] going?

Quick reply would make my day.

Davie
```

## Template 3 — Advice/feedback ask
```
Subject: Quick question

Hey [Name],

I'm exploring something in the B2B payments space and would love your take. Do you have 5 minutes for a quick call this week?

Just hit reply yes/no — no pressure either way.

Davie
```

## Template 4 — Follow-up to prior conversation
```
Subject: Following up

Hi [Name],

Wanted to follow up on our conversation about [topic]. Any updates on your end?

Reply when you can.

Davie
```

## Template 5 — Soft professional check-in
```
Subject: Checking in

Hey [Name],

It's been a while. How are things going on your end?

Would love a quick update.

Davie
```

## Rules for all warmup emails
- Subject is 2–3 words max.
- Body is 2–4 sentences.
- No links.
- No attachments.
- No "Collectly", "AR", "invoices", "AI", "automation".
- **Always ask a question the recipient will actually answer.**
- **Always include a reply prompt** like "Just hit reply" or "Reply and let me know."

## If the email lands in spam
Text or WhatsApp the person and ask them to:
1. Move the email from spam to Primary/Inbox.
2. Hit reply and send anything back.
3. Save your contact if they want.

Both actions repair reputation for `davie@getcollectly.app`.
