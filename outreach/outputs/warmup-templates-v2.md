# Warmup Email Templates v2 — mixed personal + professional contacts

No pitches. No links. No product mentions. Goal: get a reply.

## Template 1 — Pure catch-up (personal contacts)
```
Subject: Quick catch up

Hey [Name],

Hope you're doing well. What's keeping you busy these days?

Reply and let me know — always curious what you're working on.

Davie
```

## Template 2 — Specific question (personal contacts)
```
Subject: How's [company/project]?

Hi [Name],

Saw [specific thing] and thought of you. How's [company/project] going?

Quick reply would make my day.

Davie
```

## Template 5 — Soft professional check-in (personal contacts)
```
Subject: Checking in

Hey [Name],

It's been a while. How are things going on your end?

Would love a quick update.

Davie
```

## Template a1 — Curious agency ops question (DesignRush agencies)
```
Subject: Quick question

Hi there,

Saw [company] on DesignRush — strong work. I'm researching how small agencies handle the awkward part of client work: overdue invoices.

If you have 10 seconds, who's usually the one who nudges late-paying clients at your shop?

No pitch — just trying to understand how different teams handle it.

Davie
```

## Template a2 — Founder-to-founder check-in (DesignRush agencies)
```
Subject: Honest take?

Hi there,

I'm talking to a few agency founders about cashflow habits. One thing keeps coming up: clients paying 30–60 days late.

At [company], do you have a straightforward way to keep payments on track, or is it mostly manual follow-up?

Genuinely curious — one-line reply is plenty.

Davie
```

## Template a3 — Industry comparison ask (DesignRush agencies)
```
Subject: Quick agency ops question

Hey there,

I'm comparing notes with agencies on billing workflow. For [company], when an invoice goes past due, who typically chases it — the founder, project lead, or someone else?

Just collecting perspectives. A one-word answer works.

Davie
```

## Rules for all warmup emails
- Subject is 2–4 words max.
- Body is 3–5 sentences.
- No links.
- No attachments.
- No "Collectly", "AR", "AI", "automation", "software".
- Always ask a question the recipient can answer in one line.
- Always include a reply prompt.
