# 90-Second Homepage Demo Loom Script
## Collectly — for founder-led LinkedIn outreach

---

## Goal
A prospect who clicked from a LinkedIn DM watches this and thinks: "This is real, it solves my problem, and I can try it free for 14 days."

**Target length:** 85–95 seconds.
**Format:** Founder talking head (corner) + screen share of app.
**Where to use:** Homepage hero (replace "Watch 90-sec demo" button), /tour page, LinkedIn posts.

---

## SCRIPT

### OPEN — 0:00–0:10
**[Founder on camera, warm, direct]**

"If you run a small B2B service business and you’re still chasing invoices yourself, this is for you. I’m Davie, founder of Collectly. In 90 seconds I’ll show you how we automate the awkward follow-up part — without making your customers hate you."

---

### PROBLEM SETUP — 0:10–0:20
**[Switch to screen: blank QuickBooks/Xero aging report or simple slide]**

"You send an invoice. Net-30 passes. Then you have to send the 'just checking in' email. Then the firmer one. Then maybe an SMS. It’s awkward, it takes hours, and it delays your cash."

---

### SOLUTION — 0:20–0:40
**[Screen: Collectly dashboard, invoice list]**

"Collectly connects to QuickBooks or Xero in under 10 minutes. We pull your overdue invoices, and our AI sends tone-aware follow-ups — friendly at day one, firmer at day thirty, and it automatically stops the moment a customer replies or pays."

**[Show the AI dunning demo: generate a firm email]**

"You can see exactly what we’d send before it goes out. This isn’t a generic template — it adapts to the invoice age, the amount, and the tone you choose."

---

### OUTCOME — 0:40–0:55
**[Screen: branded payment portal + cash-flow forecast]**

"Your customer clicks the pay link, settles the invoice on a branded portal, and the payment gets matched back to the invoice automatically. You get a 4-week cash-flow forecast so you know if you can make payroll."

---

### TRUST + CTA — 0:55–1:25
**[Back to founder on camera]**

"No fake case studies, no invented stats. We’re onboarding 20 founding customers at $49 a month, with a 14-day free trial and no credit card. If you want to see exactly what Collectly would send your customers, click 'Start free trial' and I’ll help you set it up."

## AUDIO / DELIVERY NOTES

- **Pace:** Slightly faster than normal conversation. 90 seconds is short.
- **Tone:** Confident but not salesy. You’re showing, not convincing.
- **No buzzwords:** Avoid "AI-powered," "revolutionary," "next-gen." Use "automates," "tone-aware," "pauses when they pay."
- **Honest caveat:** If US/UK payments are still test-only, say: "Payments are live via Paystack in Africa today; for US and UK customers we set up Stripe production credentials on the first call."
