## PRODUCT
Collectly — AI-native accounts-receivable dunning tool for small B2B service businesses.

## STAGE
Live product, 0 paying customers. Blocked by 4 founder-owned setup actions: Vercel deploy, Resend domain verify, Stripe webhook secret, Twilio triplet. AI is already wired (Gemini).

## WEDGE (v1)
Local-service marketing agencies (SEO / PPC / web design) serving blue-collar and healthcare SMB clients — HVAC, plumbing, dental, med-spa, roofing, landscaping. 10–25 employees, retainer + project mix, already on QuickBooks or Xero.

## PERSONAS
- **5–15 employees:** Founder / Owner — champion + buyer, may set up integrations themselves.
- **15–30 employees:** Ops Manager / Agency Director — champion, needs founder sign-off.
- **Key influencer (underused):** Fractional bookkeeper / controller — sees aging reports weekly, serves multiple agencies, no ego stake defending the status quo.

## TRACTION
- 30 prospects contacted
- ~35 emails sent (cold + follow-up)
- 0 meaningful replies
- Stack: Apollo, Hunter, Resend, Gmail fallback

## BUDGET
$0.

## CORE THESIS
The real competitor is inertia: QuickBooks reminders + spreadsheets + founder-at-midnight shame. Chaser, Upflow, and other AR tools exist but are not the primary barrier. The question is whether agencies feel enough pain to switch to something that actually fixes the workflow.

## IDEAL SIGNALS — prove these, do not assume
1. **3+ qualified conversations from 60 touches** in the narrowed niche. Qualified = real back-and-forth about current AR handling, not "sounds cool, keep me posted."
2. **1 bookkeeper or accountant recommends it unprompted** to one of their agency clients after seeing it.
3. **1 pilot agency uses it for 2+ invoice cycles** and reports saved time or faster payment.

## NOT IDEAL SIGNALS — ordered by diagnostic strength
1. **Bookkeeper channel fails.** You contact 10 fractional bookkeepers serving agencies, offer a free AR health check, and 0–1 say "yes, I'd recommend this." If the people who see aging reports weekly and have no ego stake defending the status quo say "meh," the product is not urgent enough. **Threshold: <2 unprompted yes out of 10 = kill this channel, reassess product urgency.**
2. **Niche also silent after deliverability is fixed.** If the local-service agency niche still returns <3 qualified conversations from 60 touches with tested copy, the niche is wrong — not the message.
3. **Prospects cannot explain why QuickBooks reminders are insufficient.** If they say "we just use QBO and it's fine," your upgrade is not compelling enough to overcome inertia.

## IMMEDIATE NEXT ACTIONS
1. Fix / check Resend domain SPF / DKIM / DMARC before sending more.
2. Run A/B/C diagnostic: message vs channel vs offer on 30 touches.
3. DM 10 fractional bookkeepers with free AR health check offer.
4. Join 2 agency communities, value-first for 2 weeks.
5. Build the "$ lost to late payments" calculator as lead magnet.

## BOTTOM LINE
Plausible, not yet validated. 30 disciplined days of testing the wedge and the bookkeeper channel. If neither channel shows signal at the thresholds above, the product-urgency assumption is wrong and the wedge needs to change.
