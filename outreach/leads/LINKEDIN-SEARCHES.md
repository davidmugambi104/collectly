# LinkedIn saved searches for Mugavi (10 minutes, twice a week)

How: LinkedIn search box, paste a line, press Enter, then filters: **Posts**, **Date posted: Past week**, **Sort by: Latest**.
Do this by hand in your own account. Nothing here scrapes or automates LinkedIn. These phrases are my best guess at how
people write; keep the ones that return real people and drop the ones that return only articles and agencies.

## Bookkeepers and accountants (lead buyer)
1. `"chasing invoices" AND (bookkeeper OR bookkeeping OR accountant)`
2. `"clients not paying" OR "clients won't pay" OR "late paying clients"`
3. `"overdue invoices" AND (QuickBooks OR Xero)`
4. `"credit control" AND (bookkeeper OR "small business") NOT recruitment NOT vacancy`
5. `"payment reminders" AND (clients OR customers) AND (bookkeeping OR practice)`
6. `"accounts receivable" AND ("small business" OR "my clients") NOT "AR clerk" NOT hiring`

## Agencies and freelancers (second buyer)
7. `("unpaid invoice" OR "unpaid invoices") AND (agency OR freelancer OR consultant)`
8. `"client ghosted" OR "ghosted after delivery" OR "still hasn't paid"`
9. `"net 30" AND (client OR clients) AND ("paying late" OR "pay late" OR "slow to pay")`

## UK wording (US and UK only)
10. `"late payment" AND (UK OR HMRC OR "small business") AND ("chasing" OR "chase") NOT webinar`

## Who to keep
Keep: a person (not a company page) describing their own situation in a post or comment, in the last week, who asks a
question or vents. Skip: company pages, "7 tips" articles, vendors of competing tools (Chaser, Paidnice, Upflow,
Gaviti, BILL, Melio), recruiters, webinar promos, and anything outside the US and UK.

## What to do with a keeper
Copy the post link into `outreach/leads/runs/<date>/linkedin-inbox.txt`, one block per post, a blank line between.
Optional first line `url: https://...`; paste the post text below it. Then the LinkedIn agent drafts a 2 to 4 sentence
comment for each. You post it yourself. Comment on the post, not by DM, and do not pitch.
