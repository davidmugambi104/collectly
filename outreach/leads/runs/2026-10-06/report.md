**Draft reply** (edit into your own words):

> What you describe matches what several people in this thread see: the Automatic invoice reminders setting under Account and settings, Sales, Reminders is a separate thing from this email, so turning it off does not touch it. Until Intuit changes that, the only lever people report is Plain Text Only delivery, and you lose open tracking with it. For Net 30 customers with their own AP runs, it may be worth a short heads-up line in your invoice message saying payment follows your agreed terms and any nudge email from QuickBooks can be ignored. And since support keeps sending you to the wrong setting, ask for the case to be escalated to the product team and quote the 48 hour timing from your mock customer test.
