1. Finish Your Payment Emails Cannot Be Disabled and Are NOT Automatic Invoice Reminders

Pain point: QuickBooks emails their Net 30 customers a Finish your payment nudge two days after an invoice goes out, with reminders already off and no setting to stop it.

What you describe matches what several people in this thread see: the Automatic invoice reminders setting under Account and settings, Sales, Reminders is a separate thing from this email, so turning it off does not touch it. I can't point you to a switch for the Finish your payment email, and I don't think one exists right now. Until Intuit changes that, the only lever people report is Plain Text Only delivery, and you lose open tracking with it. For Net 30 customers with their own AP runs, it may be worth a short heads-up line in your invoice message saying payment follows your agreed terms and any nudge email from QuickBooks can be ignored. And since support keeps sending you to the wrong setting, ask for the case to be escalated to the product team and quote the 48 hour timing from your mock customer test.
