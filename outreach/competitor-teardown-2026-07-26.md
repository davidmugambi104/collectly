## 1. Chaser (chaserhq.com)

### Positioning
- **Homepage headline:** "Accounts receivable automation software"
- **Angle:** Predictable cash flow through receivables automation + forecasting.
- **Who they target:** SMBs and mid-market finance teams. Revenue-tiered pricing makes this explicit.

### Messaging
- Heavy use of "AI" and "automation" but grounded in features: workflows, templates, forecasting.
- Strong UK/AU/NZ presence with local currency pricing.
- Trust signals: G2/Capterra, case studies, 30+ templates.

### Pricing (verified live, 2026-07-26)
- **Compact:** $259/mo (US) — up to ~$5M revenue, 4 users, 30 templates, 4 workflows.
- **Core:** $779/mo — up to ~$13M revenue, unlimited users, multi-entity.
- **Complete:** $1,169/mo — up to $120M revenue, forecasting, dedicated AM.
- **Care add-on:** $447/mo — outsourced AR specialist.
- Annual discount: ~10%.

### Demo / trust
- Demo-first. No obvious free trial on homepage.
- Case studies named and linked.
- G2 badge prominent.

### What Chaser does better
1. **Feature completeness** — phone/letter channels, multi-entity, deeper forecasting.
2. **Brand trust** — 10+ years in market, named customers, reviews.
3. **Local pricing** — UK, AU, NZ, EU pricing pages.
4. **Services option** — Care lets teams fully outsource AR.

### Where Collectly should attack
1. **Price floor** — Chaser’s cheapest US plan is $259/mo. A 10-person agency paying $259/mo + $447/mo Care is $706/mo. Collectly at $49/mo is a 10×+ saving.
2. **Complexity** — Chaser has 30 templates and 4 workflows before you need a call. Collectly’s pitch is "set it in 10 minutes."
3. **Tone-aware AI** — Chaser has AI email generator; Collectly should lean into "sounds like you wrote it" differentiation.

### Recommended Collectly copy
> "Chaser is a solid tool — but it starts at $259/mo and gets expensive fast if you want forecasting or multi-entity. Collectly gives you the same AR automation for $49/mo flat, with no revenue bands and no per-invoice fees."

## 2. Kolleno (kolleno.com)

### Positioning
- **Homepage headline:** "Intelligent order-to-cash platform, built to run itself"
- **Angle:** AI agents handle collections, reconciliation, risk, disputes for CFOs.
- **Who they target:** CFOs, credit controllers, AR managers at £1M+ turnover businesses.

### Messaging
- Strongest AI-agent narrative in the space.
- "Human expertise, AI execution."
- Heavy enterprise proof: Rakuten, Drata, $4.1B revenue company, 96% DPD reduction.

### Pricing (verified live, 2026-07-26)
- **Business Pay:** £650/user/mo (~$830) — >£1M turnover.
- **Business Plus:** £1,245/user/mo (~$1,590) — >£10M turnover.
- **Enterprise / Enterprise Plus:** Custom — >£100M / £1Bn turnover.
- Onboarding: 3–8+ hours.
- Minimum users: 1–4 depending on plan.

### Demo / trust
- Demo-first. "Take a tour" secondary CTA.
- Strong video testimonials from named CFOs.
- G2 4.9/5, ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II.

### What Kolleno does better
1. **Full O2C breadth** — disputes, credit risk, cash application, reconciliation, e-invoicing.
2. **Enterprise credibility** — named big customers, certifications, reviews.
3. **AI-agent framing** — feels like a workforce, not a feature.
4. **Global sales motion** — US/UK phone numbers, localized pages.

### Where Collectly should attack
1. **Price** — Kolleno starts at ~$830/user/mo. For a 5-person team that is $4,000+/mo. Collectly is $49/mo total.
2. **Complexity** — Kolleno needs 10-day onboarding and policy uploads. Collectly is 10-minute setup.
3. **SMB neglect** — Kolleno explicitly targets >£1M turnover. The 5–50 person, sub-$5M segment is wide open.

### Recommended Collectly copy
> "Kolleno is built for CFOs at £10M+ companies with dedicated credit-control teams. If you’re a 10-person agency where the founder still chases invoices, Kolleno is overkill. Collectly is the AR-native tool built for that exact gap."

## 3. Collection.ai (collection.ai)

### Positioning
- **Homepage headline:** "Your DSO Is Costing You Millions. We Fix That."
- **Angle:** Enterprise collections infrastructure — rebuilds the gap between invoice and cash.
- **Who they target:** Large companies with $100M+ AR, liquidity timing problems.

### Messaging
- Strongest narrative clarity of any competitor.
- "Most companies think they have a revenue problem. They don’t. They have a liquidity timing problem."
- No feature list on homepage — pure enterprise consulting pitch.

### Pricing
- Not public. Enterprise sales only.

### Demo / trust
- No obvious demo CTA in fetched content.
- Lean, almost stealth-mode site.

### What Collection.ai does better
1. **Narrative** — reframes the problem as liquidity timing, not collections.
2. **Enterprise positioning** — feels like strategic infrastructure, not a tool.
3. **Simplicity** — homepage is two paragraphs, no bloat.

### Where Collectly should attack
1. **Transparency** — Collection.ai hides pricing. Collectly shows $49/mo.
2. **SMB accessibility** — Collection.ai is clearly not for 5–50 person businesses.
3. **Speed** — Collection.ai sounds like a 6-month implementation. Collectly is 10 minutes.

### Recommended Collectly copy
> "Collection.ai is rebuilding collections infrastructure for the Fortune 500. Collectly is doing the same job for the 5–50 person service business that can’t afford a 6-month enterprise rollout."

## 4. AgentCollect (agentcollect.com)

### Positioning
- **Homepage headline:** "The End of Manual Collection"
- **Angle:** AI-powered debt collection agency. Replaces traditional collection agencies.
- **Who they target:** Companies with overdue books they want outsourced; late-stage/charged-off accounts; healthcare, SaaS, fintech.

### Messaging
- Very direct: "Agencies take 25–50% commissions, damage brands, and treat debtors unfairly."
- Strong founder/credibility signals: YC Summer 2023, Checkr CEO testimonial.
- Social impact angle: erases medical debt via nonprofit partnership.

### Pricing (verified live, 2026-07-26)
- **1% of managed overdue balance** — not contingency, not success fee.
- Pilot option: no-commitment test batch.
- Custom pricing for charged-off/legal portfolios.
- Live in 20 minutes.

### Demo / trust
- Interactive voice demo on homepage (enter domain + email).
- Named testimonial: Daniel Yanisse, CEO of Checkr.
- Strong stats: 85K actions/day, 50% collection rate, 20d average collect.

### What AgentCollect does better
1. **Recovery performance** — 3.2× on hardest accounts, 50% collection rate.
2. **Voice/SMS automation** — true AI agent calls under your brand.
3. **Credibility** — YC-backed, named customers, $1M medical-debt relief story.
4. **Pricing innovation** — 1% of managed balance is easy to explain vs 25% agency cut.

### Where Collectly should attack
1. **Relationship preservation** — AgentCollect is still a collections agency, even if polite. Collectly is first-party dunning *before* accounts go late.
2. **Subscription vs contingency** — Collectly is flat-fee, predictable. AgentCollect scales with overdue balance.
3. **Pre-chase automation** — Collectly stops invoices from becoming 90-day problems. AgentCollect takes over once they already are.

### Recommended Collectly copy
> "AgentCollect replaces collection agencies for overdue accounts. Collectly prevents invoices from getting there in the first place — friendly, firm follow-ups while the invoice is still 7 or 30 days late, under your brand, for a flat $49/mo."

## Strategic implications for Collectly

### The competitive whitespace
Every named competitor either:
- Starts above $250/mo (Chaser, Kolleno), or
- Targets late-stage/charged-off accounts (AgentCollect), or
- Is enterprise-only / stealth (Collection.ai), or
- Is immature/manual (Collectify).

**Collectly’s pitch should be:**
> "The only AR-native automation tool built for 5–50 person B2B service businesses at a flat, transparent price."

### Messaging hierarchy
1. **Primary:** Stop chasing invoices. $49/mo flat.
2. **Secondary:** Built for QuickBooks/Xero agencies, consultancies, IT firms, accountants.
3. **Differentiator:** Tone-aware AI follow-ups that sound like you wrote them.
4. **Proof point:** Live demo in 90 seconds, 14-day free trial, no card.

### Pricing anchor
Use competitor pricing as an anchor in sales calls and on the website:
- Chaser: 5× more expensive at entry.
- Kolleno: 10–30× more expensive per user.
- AgentCollect: free if no recovery, but 1% scales fast and is recovery-oriented, not prevention.

### What Collectly must prove to beat them
1. **It actually gets invoices paid faster** — one real case study with named customer.
2. **Setup is really 10 minutes** — record the Loom.
3. **Founder is credible** — LinkedIn, story, response time.
4. **It does not damage customer relationships** — tone-aware demo is the core proof.
