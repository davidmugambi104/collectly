# Collectly LinkedIn Outreach Scripts v1

**Use only with official LinkedIn API or manual copy-paste. No scraping.**

## Script 1 — Connection Request (Founder-to-Founder)

Hi {{first_name}}, I run a small product that automates invoice follow-up for agencies like {{company}}. Not selling — just connecting with founders who handle A/R themselves. Worth staying in touch?

---

## Script 2 — Connection Request (Value-First)

Hi {{first_name}}, I help founder-led agencies recover late payments without the awkward chase. {{company}} looks like a great fit. Would love to connect and share what I'm seeing work in your space.

---

## Script 3 — Follow-up After Accept (Soft Pitch)

Thanks for connecting, {{first_name}}. Quick question: at {{company}}, who usually follows up when client invoices go past due — you or someone on the team?

---

## Script 4 — Follow-up After Accept (Content / Insight)

Thanks for the connect. I spend a lot of time thinking about cash flow in small agencies. Happy to share a short playbook I wrote on reducing days-sales-outstanding if it's useful.

---

## Script 5 — Re-engagement / Final Touch

Hi {{first_name}}, I know LinkedIn DMs are noisy. I'll keep this to one ask: if {{company}} ever has invoices aging past 30 days, I built something that fixes it quietly in the background. Worth a 10-min call? {{calendar_link}}
