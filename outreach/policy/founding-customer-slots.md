:# Founding Customer Slots — Template

**Status:** Draft — needs Davie's input.

Collectly needs a clear definition of "founding customer" so the outreach pipeline can prioritize and measure correctly.

## Questions to answer

1. **How many founding slots?**
   - Example: 10

2. **What do they get?**
   - Example: lifetime discounted price, direct access to founder, influence on roadmap

3. **What do they commit to?**
   - Example: 3-month pilot, monthly payment, case study/reference

4. **What is the price?**
   - Example: $49/month for 12 months, then $99/month

5. **Who qualifies?**
   - Example: US/UK/CA/AU service agency or bookkeeping firm using QBO or Xero, 5–30 employees, actively sending invoices

6. **What is the acceptance criteria?**
   - Example: connects QBO/Xero, runs Collectly for 14 days, recovers at least $500 in overdue invoices

## Why this matters

Without this definition, I cannot report:
- Founding-customer slots filled vs remaining
- Which prospects should be prioritized over others
- When a reply should be treated as a serious opportunity vs a casual conversation

## Suggested default

If you don't want to decide now, use this placeholder:

- **Slots:** 10
- **Offer:** 50% off first 6 months, founder onboarding, prioritized feature requests
- **Commitment:** 3-month pilot, agree to a public case study if results are positive
- **Price:** $49/month for 6 months, then $99/month
- **Qualification:** US/UK/CA/AU service agency or bookkeeping firm on QBO/Xero with active receivables
- **Acceptance:** Connects accounting software, recovers $500+ in first 30 days
