## New / Changed Since 2026-08-07

- **Melio: free tier renamed "Free" → "Go"; new "Platinum" tier added.** (Material.)
  - Go plan now formally branded (was just "Free") — 5 ACH/mo free, $0.50 thereafter, unlimited 2.9% card; AR & Invoicing included.
  - Per-additional-user pricing on Core/Boost now explicit: +$10/mo (or +$8/mo annual). Unlimited tier already includes unlimited seats.
  - **New: Platinum tier**, invite-only, for businesses processing $300K+/mo in card volume. Custom transaction pricing, preferred card fees, priority processing, faster settlement, white-glove account management, discounted FX, **API access**, and card-to-wire transfer options. No published price.
  - Why this matters for Collectly: Melio's bottom (Go) is still free and still bundles "Free AR & Invoicing." The new Platinum tier (with API + enterprise rails) is the first time Melio has a credible mid-market SKU. The gap between Melio-Go and Melio-Platinum is now wider, which keeps Collectly's $49–$149 sweet spot distinct.
- All other competitors: **no public delta.** Chaser, Upflow, HighRadius, Gaviti, Growfin, BILL, Versapay, QBO, Xero — all unchanged from 2026-08-07 snapshot. Growfin and Versapay `/pricing` still 404 (third consecutive run).
