## Meaningful Deltas

### 1. QBO AR — August 2026 feature drop (MEANINGFUL CHANGE)

**What changed:** QuickBooks Online published a major August 2026 update (Aug 11, 2026) with several new AR-relevant features:

- **Intuit Intelligence chat (Beta):** Admin users can now create invoices via AI chat, upload invoice data (spreadsheets, images, project documents), and put invoicing on autopilot. This is AI-driven invoice creation — not just reminders.
- **Custom automations via Intuit Intelligence:** Users can build, configure, schedule, and run automations that generate reports, send reminders, or summarize data. This is workflow automation that overlaps with AR automation tools.
- **Conversational business intelligence:** Instant insights, scenario comparison, and planning through Intuit Intelligence — overlapping with cash-flow forecasting positioning.
- **Bill Pay Elite now included with QBO Advanced:** Standard ACH payments through Bill Pay are now **free**. This removes the per-ACH fee barrier that BILL ($0.59/ACH) and Melio ($0.50/ACH after quota) capitalize on.
- **QuickBooks Payments through Claude and ChatGPT:** Businesses can now create and send estimates, invoices, payment links, and reminders from Claude or ChatGPT. AI drafts require approval before sending.
- **Management reports in QBO Advanced:** Full financial reporting suite with custom management/industry-specific reports, self-serve analytics, and AI-powered forecasting.
- **Expert books upkeep:** AI + expert-verified automated transaction posting service.

**Why it matters:** QBO is the accounting system many Collectly prospects already use. These features directly encroach on Collectly's value proposition in three ways:
1. **AI invoicing on autopilot** — QBO now handles invoice creation via AI, which was previously a manual step Collectly complemented.
2. **Custom reminder automations** — QBO can now schedule and send reminders as custom automations, narrowing the gap with Collectly's AI dunning.
3. **Free ACH in Bill Pay Elite** — removes a cost pain point that Collectly's payment features address. If QBO Advanced users get free ACH natively, the "more than Melio, for the price of BILL" positioning weakens for QBO-native prospects.

**Action:** Update /vs-qbo collateral to address:
- QBO's AI invoicing is Beta, admin-only, limited queries — Collectly's AI dunning is production-grade, multi-channel, and autonomous.
- QBO's custom automations are generic workflow builders, not collections-specific. Collectly's AI pauses on payment or reply — QBO's automations don't.
- Free ACH in Bill Pay Elite is only for QBO Advanced subscribers. Collectly's payment features work across QBO and Xero.
- Claude/ChatGPT integration is a novelty channel, not a full AR workflow. Collectly handles the full collections lifecycle.
