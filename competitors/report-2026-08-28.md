## Headline

> **⚠️ MEANINGFUL CHANGE — Xerocon Denver (Aug 19-20) announced Xero Payment Follow Ups: JAX-powered, behaviour-adaptive collections that switches from email to SMS based on customer payment behaviour.** This is the most significant Xero AR development to date and the first direct overlap with Collectly's AI dunning value prop. Also announced: **Casper** (Melio AI client manager that works across all ledgers), **Xero Bill Payments** (pay bills inside Xero via Melio), and **Melio Expense Management**. Payment Follow Ups is "coming soon" — not yet shipping — but the trajectory is clear: Xero is building native AR collections automation.

## What Changed (Xero AR — meaningful)

1. **Payment Follow Ups, powered by JAX** [coming soon] — JAX analyzes each customer's unique payment history and automatically executes tailored collection plans. Adapts approach: consolidating invoices, sending timed nudges, or **switching to SMS** for clients who ignore email. This is the first Xero-native feature that directly competes with Collectly's AI dunning.
2. **Casper** — Melio's AI-powered client manager for accounting firms. Proactively identifies missing information, contacts clients, collects documents. **Works across every ledger** (not just Xero) — targets QuickBooks practices too. Complements JAX. Not a collections tool (it's a document/info chase tool), but overlaps with the "automated client communication" space.
3. **Xero Bill Payments** — Bills can now be paid from inside Xero, powered by Melio. Closes the AP gap in Xero's US stack.
4. **Melio Expense Management** — Real-time card expense tracking/categorization, accounting-agnostic (works with any platform).
5. **Melio API** [closed beta] — Direct access to Melio's payment rails (ACH, RTP, checks, wires, virtual cards).
6. **JAX Scenario Planning** — Model price rises, cost increases, hiring decisions against real Xero data.
7. **XeroForce month-end agent** — Pre-built agent for document/reconciliation status through to prepayment/amortization journals.
8. **Melio CEO Matan Bar named Xero US CEO** — signals deep Xero-Melio integration strategy.
