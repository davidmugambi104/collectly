## Meaningful Deltas

### 1. HighRadius — outcome-based pricing model confirmed (NEW)

**What changed:** ToolRadar (updated Aug 18, 2026) confirms HighRadius now offers a formal outcome-based pricing model: "Pay only when AI agents deliver value — Mutually Agreed Success Criteria (MASC), Implementation fees: $0 till go-live, Gain share post go-live." KPI guarantees: 10% DSO reduction, 50% idle cash reduction, 30% faster financial close, 40% productivity increase.

**Why it matters:** This is a go-to-market shift from "custom enterprise pricing" to "pay-for-outcomes." It signals confidence in their AI agents' ability to deliver measurable results — and it changes the competitive conversation. When a prospect mentions HighRadius, the counter is: "Collectly delivers the same outcomes (DSO reduction, AI dunning, automation) without the enterprise implementation cycle or gain-share contract."

**Action:** Add outcome-based pricing angle to sales enablement. Update `/vs-highradius` if it exists; otherwise flag for creation.

**Action:** Update /vs-bill collateral to reflect $0.59 ACH.

### 3. Xero-Melio acquisition context captured (NEW)

**What changed:** checkthat.ai (last updated March 30, 2026) reports Xero announced acquisition of Melio for $2.5 billion in June 2025, targeted completion within 6 months. Pricing remains unchanged through March 2026. This context was not in previous snapshots.

**Why it matters:** Xero now owns a free AR/AP platform (Melio). While pricing is unchanged through March 2026, post-acquisition integration could eventually converge Xero AR + Melio capabilities — potentially making Xero a stronger AR competitor if Melio's features get bundled into Xero plans. This is a structural competitive landscape shift, not an immediate pricing change. Watch for integration announcements.

**Action:** Add to watch list. Monitor for post-acquisition integration news.
