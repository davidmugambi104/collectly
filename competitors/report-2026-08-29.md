## What Changed (Xero AR — meaningful)

1. **Xero Payment Follow Ups — appears to have shipped (email-only).** Xero Central help article "Set up payment follow-ups" now live with setup instructions. JAX sends email reminders, combines multiple invoices into one email, can be paused/resumed/stopped manually. This is the first Xero-native AR collections feature available to users.
2. **SMS not yet documented.** Xerocon Denver (Aug 19-20) announced email→SMS adaptation based on customer payment behaviour, but the help article only mentions email. SMS likely shipping in a later phase.
3. **No automatic pause-on-payment-or-reply mentioned.** The help article describes manual pause/resume/stop — no indication of automatic pause when a customer pays or replies. This remains a key Collectly differentiator.
4. **Bill Protection also announced** at Xerocon Denver (reviews bills before payment, flags unusual amounts/bank changes/unfamiliar suppliers) — not yet in help documentation.
