## Positioning Headlines to Use

1. **"Big-tool power, small-business price"** — Chaser is $259/mo at the floor; Collectly starts at $49/mo.
2. **"Built for the 5–50 segment everyone ignores"** — HighRadius and Gaviti target 500+; Quadient targets small but is manual; Chaser and Upflow are quote-based and indirect.
3. **"Paystack + QBO + Xero in one place"** — unique to Africa + global SMBs.
4. **"AI dunning that pauses on payment or reply"** — explicit differentiator vs basic email reminders.
5. **"Cash-flow forecast tells you when payroll is safe"** — direct hit on the SMB cash pain.

## Competitors to Watch (priority order)
