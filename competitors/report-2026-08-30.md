## Headline

> **⚠️ MEANINGFUL CHANGE — Xero JAX now supports WhatsApp and SMS as channels.** JAX release notes (published Aug 21, 2026) confirm: "you can create invoices in different currencies using JAX within Xero, via SMS, WhatsApp and email." AccountingToday (Aug 24, 2026) confirms Payment Follow Ups uses adaptive channel selection — sending SMS to clients who ignore emails, and email to clients who ignore texts — based on payment behavior. This narrows Collectly's multi-channel advantage. **However, Xero still lacks automatic pause-on-payment-or-reply** (only manual pause/resume/stop), which remains Collectly's key differentiator.

## What Changed (Xero AR — meaningful)

1. **JAX now supports SMS, WhatsApp, and email as channels.** JAX release notes page (central.xero.com, published Aug 21, 2026) confirms invoice creation via SMS, WhatsApp, and email. This is the first documented WhatsApp support in Xero's AR workflow.
2. **Payment Follow Ups confirmed using adaptive SMS/email.** AccountingToday (Aug 24, 2026) reports from Xerocon Denver: JAX "looks at the customer's past payment behavior — how they pay, when they pay, how often they pay — and then builds a unique plan for each customer. Someone else always ignores texts but checks emails, and so gets an email statement, while someone else who ignores their emails will get a text message instead."
3. **Aug 29 snapshot said "SMS not yet documented" — now upgraded to SMS and WhatsApp confirmed.** The Aug 29 snapshot correctly noted the help article only mentioned email. The JAX release notes page and AccountingToday coverage now confirm SMS is shipping as part of Payment Follow Ups, and WhatsApp is a JAX channel.
4. **No automatic pause-on-payment-or-reply mentioned.** Neither the JAX release notes, the help article, nor the AccountingToday presentation mention automatic pause when a customer pays or replies. Manual pause/resume/stop remains the only option. **This remains Collectly's primary differentiator.**
5. **XeroForce, Smart Doc Capture, Xero Partner Hub enhancements** also confirmed in AccountingToday — month-end agent, document chasing, live book health. Not directly AR-competitive but expand Xero's AI footprint.

**Source:** Xero Central JAX release notes (central.xero.com/s/article/Release-notes-for-Just-Ask-Xero-JAX, published Aug 21, 2026), AccountingToday (Aug 24, 2026), Xerocon London media release (Jul 9, 2026).
