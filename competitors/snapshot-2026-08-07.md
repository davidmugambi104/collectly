## New / Changed Since 2026-08-04

- **Melio: AP → AP+AR.** Now publishes "Free AR & Invoicing" on every plan tier. Closest free-tier competitor to Collectly. (Material.)
- **BILL: +$4–$10 per seat.** Per-user pricing increased on all paid tiers. Weakens the "BILL is cheap for small teams" argument. (Material.)
- **Chaser: new "Care" add-on.** Human AR specialist service at $324/mo GBP / $447 USD. First managed-service SKU. (Notable.)
- **Versapay: pricing page removed.** No public delta; suggests tighter sales qualification. (Incidental.)
- **Growfin: pricing page removed.** Same as Versapay. (Incidental.)
- **Xero: dedicated AR landing page.** Marketing rebrand of existing recurring-invoice + reminder features. (Incidental.)
- **HighRadius messaging:** "guaranteed operational KPI improvements, not AI hype" — slight counter-positioning to AI-agent hype cycle. (Notable.)
