# Collectly Competitor Report — 2026-08-07

**Compared against:** `collectly/competitors/snapshot-2026-08-04.md`

## Headline

> **Melio has entered AR.** They now bundle "Free AR & Invoicing" on every plan, including the free tier, and have published paid Core/Boost/Unlimited tiers ($25/$55/$80 monthly). Melio is no longer an AP-only story — they're the closest free-tier competitor to Collectly on the SMB invoice + collection loop.

## Meaningful Deltas

### 1. Melio — material positioning shift (AP → AP+AR)

- **2026-08-04 baseline:** "AP only, no AR/invoicing; Free (ACH) and 2.9% card."
- **Today:** Free plan adds "Free AR & Invoicing — Create invoices, get paid by card or bank, create payment links." Paid tiers Core ($25/mo), Boost ($55/mo), and Unlimited ($80/mo) extend this with branded invoices, QBO + Xero + NetSuite sync, batch payments, and an AI assistant. Per-user fee drops from baseline assumptions (free → Core $25 is far cheaper than Chaser's $259/mo).
- **Why it matters for Collectly:** Melio is the only competitor that overlaps Collectly's bottom-of-market pricing AND now offers an AR path. They will likely show up alongside Collectly in any "free invoicing + reminders" comparison search. Collectly's edge: tone-aware AI dunning, multi-currency day one, Paystack/Africa support — none of which Melio advertises.

### 2. BILL — per-seat price increase across the board

- **2026-08-04 baseline:** Essentials $45, Team $55, Corporate $79 (per user/mo).
- **Today:** Essentials **$49**, Team **$65**, Corporate **$89** (per user/mo). Enterprise remains custom.
- **Why it matters for Collectly:** A 5-person SMB on BILL Team just moved from $275/mo to $325/mo; on Corporate from $395 to $445. The "BILL is cheap because it's per-user" argument is weaker now. Collectly's flat $49–$149 story has slightly widened the price gap at the small-team floor.

### 3. Chaser — no public change (re-verified)

- Compact $259/mo, Core $779/mo, Complete $1,169/mo — all unchanged.
- New add-on: "Care" service tier ($324/mo GBP / $447 USD) — a human AR-specialist layer on top of Chaser software. First time we've seen Chaser position services + software in a single SKU. Not a price change, but a new competitive vector: SMBs who want human help can get it from Chaser; Collectly's positioning should highlight fully self-serve automation vs. Chaser's optional managed service.

### 4. Upflow — no public change (re-verified)

- Still quote-based. Tier names unchanged: Discover (free) / Grow ($0–10M ARR) / Scale ($10–50M) / Strategic ($50M+).

### 5. Gaviti — no public change (re-verified)

- Pricing page still "Request a Quote" with usage-based messaging. New platform modules referenced in copy: AI assistant + agentic AI chat, customer-facing payment portal, dispute/claim workflows. Worth a deeper read next run if we want to compare on AI-agent features.

### 6. HighRadius — no public change (re-verified)

- Order-to-Cash page emphasizes "guaranteed operational KPI improvements, not AI hype" (20% lower past dues, 30% productivity lift, 2X collection calls). $0 implementation-fee message still implicit in mid-market positioning. Custom pricing unchanged.

### 7. Growfin — pricing page no longer exists

- `/pricing/` returns 404. Baseline listed "Custom" anyway, so no price delta, but a published pricing page going away usually means the vendor is tightening sales qualification. Worth noting for the next sales-battlecard update.

### 8. Versapay — pricing page no longer exists

- `/pricing` returns 404. Baseline listed "Custom." No public delta. Versapay homepage is now organized around a "Platform overview" / "Interactive Platform Tours" IA — that self-serve product tour is new-ish positioning worth flagging for our next `/vs-versapay` page.

### 9. QBO / Xero built-in AR

- **QuickBooks Online:** invoice reminder automation is still present and well-documented (Intuit help article dated 2026-08-05). Reminder scheduling, recurring invoices, view/payment tracking — all unchanged.
- **Xero:** Xero now publishes a dedicated **"Accounts Receivable Automation Software"** landing page (`xero.com/us/accounting-software/accounts-receivable/`) positioning their 30-day cash-flow projection, recurring invoices, and polite reminders as AR automation. This is a marketing rebrand of existing features, not a new capability. Xero plans (US) show a heavy first-6-months discount: Early $2.50 → $25, Growing $5.50 → $55, Established $9 → $90.
