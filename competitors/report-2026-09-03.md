## What Changed

### 1. QBO — Subscription price increases + major AI/AR feature expansion ⚠️

**Price changes (effective Aug 1, 2026 renewals):**
- QBO Essentials, Plus, and Advanced: **prices increasing** for renewals on/after Aug 1, 2026
- QBO Free, Lite, Ledger, and Simple Start: **unchanged**
- Intuit says increases support continued AI investment
- Exact new prices not published on the price-changes page — varies by subscription and renewal date

**New features (August 2026 release, confirmed via Intuit product page + Squire Sep 1 article):**
- **Invoicing on Autopilot (beta)** — AI drafts invoices from scanned documents (SOWs, sales orders), sends prioritized payment reminders with custom rules. Available in Essentials, Plus, Advanced. This is directly AR/collections-relevant.
- **Auto Categorization** — AI handles transaction categorization (Plus and Advanced)
- **Conversational Business Intelligence** — plain-language questions about business finances ("What was my cash flow last month?")
- **KPIs and Dashboards** — now in Essentials and Plus (previously Advanced-only)
- **Enhanced Bank Feeds** — AI-powered batch controls and inline editing
- **Intelligent Onboarding** — hybrid AI + human guidance
- **ChatGPT/Claude integration expanded** — sales quote-to-cash workflows, deeper payroll queries, QuickBooks Capital lending insights

**Collectly impact:** QBO's Invoicing on Autopilot now overlaps more directly with Collectly's AI dunning. However: (a) it's still Beta, (b) custom rules ≠ automatic pause-on-payment-or-reply, (c) email-only (no SMS/WhatsApp), (d) QBO-only (no Xero or Paystack). Price increases may push QBO users to evaluate alternatives. Position Collectly as production-grade, multi-channel, cross-platform.

### 2. Gaviti — Significant feature expansion to full Invoice-to-Cash platform ⚠️

**New modules (confirmed via direct web_fetch of gaviti.com homepage):**
- **Credit & Risk Management** — online credit applications, AI-powered credit review, credit searches in 200+ countries, credit bureau integrations, automated credit limit setting, enforceable credit limits, credit utilization flagging, client order freezes
- **AI Assistant** — agentic A/R assistant (AI chat for the team), optimized dunning emails, credit decision support, remittance matching confidence
- **Dispute Management** — now prominently featured as a dedicated module (automated routing, trend analysis)

Gaviti now positions as a 7-module "Invoice-to-Cash Platform": Credit & Risk Management, Collections Automation, Customer Self-Service Portal, AI Cash Application, Dispute Management, AI Assistant, Analytics & Forecasting.

**No pricing change** — still quote-based, usage-based, Standard/Pro/Expert.

**Collectly impact:** Gaviti is moving upstream and broadening. Credit management is a different problem than collections, but the expanded surface area makes Gaviti more competitive for mid-market deals that want an all-in-one. Key Collectly differentiators remain: cross-platform (QBO + Xero + Paystack), automatic pause-on-payment-or-reply, self-serve onboarding, simpler pricing.
