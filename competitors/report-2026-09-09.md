## Impact on Collectly positioning

1. **Xero moves to #1 on the watch list** (from #4). Xero now owns Melio and is bundling payments + ACH into its accounting plans. This reduces the standalone value proposition of third-party AR tools that integrate with Xero — if Xero gives away free ACH and AI-powered bank reconciliation, Collectly's value must be clearly beyond what Xero bundles.

2. **Collectly's key differentiator remains intact:** pause-on-payment-or-reply. Neither Xero's automated reminders nor QBO's Invoicing on Autopilot (beta) mention this feature. This is still the #1 differentiator.

3. **New positioning headline added (#15):** "Xero owns Melio now — but Xero's AR reminders still don't pause on payment or reply. Collectly does, across both QBO and Xero."

4. **Melio as a standalone competitor is now contextual.** Melio's pricing page is still live and unchanged, but Melio is now a Xero subsidiary. Collectly competes with Melio's AR features indirectly through Xero now.

5. **Upflow's $333/mo Starter with $0 onboarding and unlimited seats** is more accessible than before. Watch whether this pulls SMBs away from Collectly. Still no self-serve signup, still no pause-on-payment-or-reply.
