## Headline

> **Melio added a Platinum tier and rebranded its free plan as "Go".** Melio now publishes a 5-tier ladder (Go free → Core $25 → Boost $55 → Unlimited $80 → Platinum invite-only). The new Platinum tier is the first Melio SKU with **API access** and **card-to-wire transfer options**, targeted at businesses processing $300K+/month in card volume. This widens the gap between Melio-Go (free, thin AR) and Melio-Platinum (enterprise rails, custom pricing) — keeping Collectly's $49–$149 flat-rate sweet spot distinct.

## Meaningful Deltas

### 1. Melio — Platinum tier + "Go" rebrand (material)

- **2026-08-07 baseline:** Free plan + Core $25 / Boost $55 / Unlimited $80. All tiers bundled "Free AR & Invoicing."
- **Today:**
  - Free plan rebranded to **"Go"** (still $0/free forever; 5 ACH/mo free then $0.50 each; unlimited 2.9% card).
  - Per-additional-user pricing now explicit on Core/Boost: **+$10/mo** (or **+$8/mo** annual).
  - Unlimited plan: still $80/mo, still includes unlimited seats.
  - **New tier: Platinum (invite-only).** For businesses processing **$300K+/month in card payments**. Custom transaction pricing, preferred card fees, priority processing, faster settlement, white-glove account management, discounted FX, **API access**, **card-to-wire transfer options**.
- **Why it matters for Collectly:**
  - Melio-Go still compresses the bottom of the market (free AR), but the *first* Melio tier with API and enterprise rails is Platinum — there's now a big gap between "free thin reminders" and "API + card-to-wire at custom pricing." Collectly's $49–$149 flat rate fills exactly that gap.
  - If a prospect asks "which competitor has an API for custom integrations?", the honest answer is now "Melio-Platinum (invite-only)" — Collectly needs to know whether it has its own API story or whether to lean on QBO/Xero integration depth instead.
  - The Xero acquisition of Melio (announced June 2025) means there's a credible path to a bundled "Xero + Melio Payments" premium tier in late 2026 / early 2027 (per Melio investor briefings). No pricing announced yet — monitor.
